隐私说明

本说明适用于 loontax.vercel.app 的候补名单。LoonTax 目前由个人创始人运营，可以通过联系表单联系我们。

我们收集什么

  • 你在登记表里填写的内容：邮箱、使用的券商、是否打算购买、是否是报税师，以及你同意接收通知的时间。
  • 你通过联系表单发来的内容：邮箱和留言。
  • 访问统计：打开页面时记录访问的页面、渠道标记（如链接里的 ?src=）、页面语言和来源网站的域名。不使用 cookie，我们的数据库不保存你的 IP 地址。

用来做什么

  • 在产品上线时给你发邮件，告诉你上线时间和早鸟购买方式；
  • 回复你的留言；
  • 统计哪些券商需求最大、哪些渠道有效，决定先做什么。

我们不会发送与 LoonTax 上线无关的推广邮件，不会出售或转让你的信息。

存在哪里

数据存放在 Supabase 的加拿大中部（蒙特利尔）机房。网站由 Vercel 托管，Vercel 会按其政策保存常规的服务器访问日志。

保存多久

产品上线并完成早鸟购买通知后 12 个月内删除未购买用户的登记信息；你也可以随时要求提前删除。

你的权利

  • 退订：在 退订页面 输入邮箱，或点我们发出的任何邮件里的退订链接。
  • 查看、更正或删除：通过联系表单告诉我们，我们会在 30 天内处理。

如对我们处理个人信息的方式有疑虑，你也可以联系加拿大隐私专员办公室（Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada）。

关于未来的产品

上线后的报告工具在你的浏览器里完成计算，交易文件不会上传到我们的服务器。具体细节会在上线时的隐私说明里写明。

Privacy notice

This notice covers the waitlist on loontax.vercel.app. LoonTax is currently run by its individual founder. Reach us through the contact form.

What we collect

  • What you enter in the waitlist form: your email, the brokers you use, whether you'd buy, whether you're a tax preparer, and when you consented to be emailed.
  • What you send through the contact form: your email and message.
  • Visit statistics: the page visited, a channel tag (such as ?src= in the link), page language and the referring site's domain. No cookies. Our database does not store your IP address.

Why

  • To email you at launch with the launch date and how to buy at the early-bird price;
  • To reply to your messages;
  • To see which brokers and channels matter most, so we build the right thing first.

We won't send promotional email unrelated to the LoonTax launch, and we never sell or share your information.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase in its Canada Central (Montréal) region. The site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server access logs under its own policy.

How long

We delete waitlist entries of people who don't buy within 12 months after launch and the early-bird notice. You can ask us to delete yours sooner at any time.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe: enter your email on the unsubscribe page, or use the link in any email we send.
  • Access, correct or delete: tell us through the contact form and we'll respond within 30 days.

If you have concerns about how we handle personal information, you can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

About the future product

The report tool will do its calculations in your browser; your trade files won't be uploaded to our servers. Details will be in the privacy notice published at launch.
