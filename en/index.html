Why tax season hurts

Your broker's slips usually aren't enough

01

T5008 cost is often blank or wrong

Canada uses average cost (ACB). After transfers between brokers, DRIPs and multiple accounts, the broker's number rarely matches. You have to work it out yourself.

02

Rebuy within 30 days and the loss is denied

Buy the same security within 30 days before or after the sale — including in your spouse's or your TFSA / RRSP accounts — and the loss becomes a superficial loss, added to the new position's cost.

03

Every US trade needs converting to CAD

Buys and sells are each converted at their own date's rate, and the currency move itself creates a gain or loss. Looking up rates for hundreds of trades by hand is a recipe for mistakes.

FAQ

Questions you might have

Isn't Wealthsimple Tax free?

Yes, and it imports Wealthsimple's own accounts automatically. But if you trade at Questrade, IBKR or elsewhere, you still have to enter capital gains yourself. LoonTax works out those numbers.

Is my data safe?

All calculation happens in your browser. Your trade files are never uploaded to our servers, and we never ask for broker passwords.

What about TFSA / RRSP trades?

Trades inside registered accounts aren't reported as capital gains, but they can trigger a superficial loss. LoonTax checks them for that and leaves them out of taxable gains.

Does it replace an accountant?

No, and it isn't meant to. It does the most time-consuming math and leaves a traceable trail. For complex situations — corporate accounts, options, cross-border residency — talk to a professional.

Why February 2027?

Brokers send tax slips by the end of February, and ETF distribution breakdowns (including ROC) are published early in the year. Accurate results need that data.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If the report isn't useful to you, contact us within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
