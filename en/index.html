FAQ

Questions you might have

How is this different from Wealthsimple?

Wealthsimple sees only its own accounts, and its automatic tax-loss harvesting is for some of its clients only. LoonTax puts trades from Questrade, IBKR and your other accounts — TFSA and RRSP included — in one ledger, so it catches superficial losses from a rebuy in another account.

Is my data safe?

All calculation happens in your browser. Your trade files are never uploaded to our servers, and we never ask for broker passwords.

What about TFSA / RRSP trades?

They aren’t reported as capital gains, but they can trigger a superficial loss. LoonTax checks them for that and leaves them out of taxable gains.

Will the year-end check tell me what to sell?

No. It shows what selling today would mean for your taxes, not what to buy or sell. That decision is yours, or a licensed advisor’s.

Does it replace an accountant?

No. It does the time-consuming math and leaves a traceable trail. For corporate accounts, options or cross-border residency, talk to a professional.

Why does the tax report wait until February 2027?

Brokers send slips by the end of February, and ETF distribution breakdowns (return of capital included) come out early in the year. Accurate results need that data.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If the report isn’t useful, contact us within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
